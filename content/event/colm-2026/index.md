summary: Training language models to construct ASCII layouts improves their spatial reasoning from text alone.
abstract: 'We introduce Text2Space, a dataset that pairs natural language descriptions with ground-truth ASCII grid layouts, separating failures in constructing spatial representations from failures in reasoning over them. Language models read these layouts well but struggle to construct them from text, and training models to construct layouts improves spatial reasoning from text alone, with gains that transfer to three external benchmarks.'
